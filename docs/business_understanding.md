# Business Understanding

## Problem Statement

Home Credit aims to provide loans to individuals with limited or no traditional credit history.

The challenge is determining whether an applicant is likely to repay the loan while minimizing financial risk and ensuring fair access to credit.

---

## Business Objective

Build an intelligent decision-support platform that predicts the probability of loan default and assists loan officers by providing explainable recommendations rather than only binary approval decisions.

---

## Machine Learning Objective

Predict the TARGET variable.

TARGET = 1 → Client experienced payment difficulties.

TARGET = 0 → Client successfully repaid the loan.

---

## Success Criteria

• High ROC-AUC

• High Recall for risky applicants

• Low False Approval Rate

• Explainable predictions

• Fast inference

• Human-readable recommendations

---

## End Users

• Loan Officers

• Risk Analysts

• Branch Managers

• Customers

• Executive Management
